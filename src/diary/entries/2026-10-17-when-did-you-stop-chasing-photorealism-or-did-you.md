---
title: "When did you stop chasing photorealism — or did you?"
date: 2026-10-17
---

I never stopped chasing it.

A friend of mine in the TouchDesigner community works on world models and has been doing realism-based generation for a couple of years now. He is the one who inspired me to keep searching for that uber-realistic generation. Getting all the way there takes the premium models, but we run MiniMax H3 now and it is pretty good, and so much of it comes down to the prompt.

In my own visual work there have always been elements of realism. I used to do caricature, realistic but cartoonish. My paintings are very abstract, but the realism is still in there, hidden underneath the colour and the theory and the principles behind them.

When it comes to AI, I think realism is the chase, and the chase is real. You look at Midjourney output and it is creative and different from reality, and at the same time it can look completely real, the same way high-end CGI can. There is the deepfake question hanging over all of it, with its pros and cons.

A film camera recording a scene looks real because it is reality. Anything computer generated has to emulate that, and AI is the closest a consumer has ever been able to get to that kind of output. In my own creativity, I like to ride the line: realism embedded into abstraction.
